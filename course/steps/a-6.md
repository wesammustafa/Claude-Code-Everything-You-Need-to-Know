## intro
- minutes: "about 20 minutes"
- why: "give every teammate the same Claude Code kit from git, and let each of them opt out without a commit"
- can: "commit the settings everyone should get, keep your personal files out of git, and give the team a plugin that loads from a marketplace inside the repository"
- payoff: "commit the settings everyone should get, keep your personal files out of git, and give the team a plugin that loads from a marketplace inside the repository"
- read: "[A team's shared settings](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/settings-example#a-teams-shared-settings): a complete committed settings file to compare with yours."
- need: "bash, `curl` and git, on macOS, Linux or WSL 2."
- need-repo: "Your own repository with your Intermediate setup"
